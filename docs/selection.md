Onspring annual bill evidence: a US$33,808 buyer-reported median for the overall platform, reported by [ComplianceOfficer](https://complianceofficer.ai/blog/onspring-pricing) from Vendr on 22 September 2026. Direct Vendr retrieval returned an access error. This is secondary evidence, not an observed NZ/AU invoice and not a continuity-only price. Onspring's [official pricing](https://onspring.com/platform/pricing/) publishes licensing structure but no amount. The public comparison uses no dollar amounts and prices is an empty list.

Document Locator and Device42 also have quote-only vendor pricing. ParaSolution's directory starting price did not establish a five-figure bill. Their annual-bill score is conservative because current primary evidence is absent, not a claim that the product costs less. Device42 was also rejected because automated discovery is a major part of its value and would expand the build beyond a useful records-based system.

Local customer context searches found no named Onspring user. No live CRM evidence was available in this no-secret build. Buyer fit is 2, not an assertion that a current customer owns the tool. Search is a qualitative score from the presence of pricing and alternatives results; no measured keyword volume was obtained.

The saved report import is supported by the [vendor-owned SDK](https://github.com/onspring-technologies/onspring-api-sdk). Its report model contains columns and rows of cells with source record IDs. Application fields still need mapping. No claim of a universal one-click export or complete suite replacement is made.
